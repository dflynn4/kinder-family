The Family Tree

Alan is at the top. Under each person are their parents, so the further down you scroll, the further back in time you go. Everything here is proven by a real record unless it's tagged otherwise.

Tags: Probable means a strong bet that isn't nailed down yet. Unproven means a guess with no real record behind it.

  • The veteranAlan James Kinder born 1925The WWII sound-ranging man this whole site is about. Born in a Washington coal town.
    • His fatherDarrell W. Kinder 1899–1977Grew up on the Waitsburg wheat ranch, then moved west to the coal-and-timber country south of Seattle.
      • His fatherJames Buchanan Kinder 1859–1945Born on the Oregon land claim; named for the president. Died just weeks after Alan came home from the war. The only ancestor whose face survives, from his 1946 obituary.
        • His fatherFranklin Peter Kinder 1829–1889Virginia, then the California Gold Rush, then the Oregon Trail in 1852. Claimed the free government land.
          • His father?Christian Kinder about 1804 UnprovenThe online trees name him as Franklin's father, but no record proves it. This is where the solid Kinder proof runs out. German Kinders came to Virginia in the 1700s, but nothing ties Alan's line to them.
        • His motherRebecca Isabell Evans 1835–1912Married Franklin in Missouri and crossed the trail with him.
          • Her fatherThomas H. Evans 1808–1869Kentucky to Missouri to Oregon, one step at a time.
      • His motherElla Muncy 1874–1912Born in Oregon. Died young, of cancer, when her son Darrell was only twelve.
        • Her fatherIsaac Newton Muncy born 1844Frontier preacher who later sat in the Oregon state legislature.
    • His motherVera May Deye 1899–1995A schoolteacher, born in Michigan. Married Darrell in Walla Walla in 1923.
      • Her fatherCharles William Deye 1868–1946Born in Shelby County, Ohio. The Deyes turned out to be an old New Jersey family, not German.
        • His fatherJacob Dey 1834–1898The oldest Deye we can prove, born in Ohio. Spelled his name "Dey."
          • His fatherJohn Wesley Dey 1810–1851 ProbableBorn in New Jersey. Brought the family out to the Ohio farm country around 1830.
            • His fatherLewis Dey born 1779 ProbableBorn in Freehold, New Jersey; married Mary Baird. An old colonial New Jersey family, here since the 1600s.
      • Her motherLou May North 1876–1964Born in Hampton, Iowa. Gave Vera her middle name, May.
        • Her fatherDaniel Martin North 1843–1907A Civil War corporal, Company H, 32nd Iowa Infantry. Fought at Pleasant Hill, Nashville, and the storming of Mobile.
          • His fatherLayton North 1809–1851Born in Ohio, died young in Indiana. His gravestone and marriage record both survive.
            • His parentsJohn North & Tamar Mendenhall early 1800s ProbableEnglish Quaker stock. The Mendenhall name may tie into a famous Quaker family that runs back to 1600s Wiltshire, England, but one link is still missing.

Two big branches, both reaching about the same depth. On Dad's side, the Kinders, Evanses, and Muncys were all Oregon Trail pioneers. On Mom's side, the Deyes were German farmers out of Ohio and the Norths were an old English-stock family with a Civil War soldier. It all comes together with Alan.
